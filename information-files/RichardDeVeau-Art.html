Painter · Novelist · Creative Director

Artist’s Statement

For me, painting is breathing... inhale ideas, ideologies and passions, exhale them onto canvas, paper, and wood.

Exploring unusual materials and experimental techniques that create unique surface texture, line, form and color is an ongoing part of my process.

I tend to work in a series manner, delving into a method or theme for a period of time, usually until it plays itself out. This  can range from weeks to months, or sometimes years, before I move on to whatever is next.

With each new series, I tend to bring along something from the work that preceded it.

​Over time, I've created my own visual vocabulary and voice.

About

BatavaiIL.co was created to build a stronger relationship between the residents of Batavia with the businesses and organizations that serve this community using the medium of short videos.

The facilitators of these videos and connections are the youth of this community: Batavia High School students. 

They are mentored by Gary Ricke, owner of a local Marketing Agency which specializes in helping small businesses with limited time and budgets grow their businesses with new, innovative marketing techniques.
